# Practical Examples

## Softmax Regression

> A classification task where each input is assigned to `1` of `N` categories.

Compared with logistic regression:
| logistic regression<br/>(2 possible categories) | softmax regression<br/>(3 possible categories) |
| - | -|
| $z=\vec{w}\cdot\vec{x}+b$<br/>$a_1=g(z)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-z}}=P(y=1\|\vec{x})$<br/>$a_2=1-a_1=P(y=0\|\vec{x})$ | $z_1=\vec{w_1}\cdot\vec{x}+b_1$, $a_1=\frac{e^{z_1}}{e^{z_1}+e^{z_2}+e^{z_3}}=P(y=1\|\vec{x})$<br/>$z_2=\vec{w_2}\cdot\vec{x}+b_2$, $a_2=\frac{e^{z_2}}{e^{z_1}+e^{z_2}+e^{z_3}}=P(y=2\|\vec{x})$<br/>$z_3=\vec{w_3}\cdot\vec{x}+b_3$, $a_3=\frac{e^{z_3}}{e^{z_1}+e^{z_2}+e^{z_3}}=P(y=3\|\vec{x})$ |

**Model**

$$
\begin{aligned}
& z_j = \vec{w_j} \cdot \vec{x} + b_j & j = 1,...,N \\
& a_j = \frac{e^{z_j}}{\sum_{k=1}^{N} e^{z_k}}=P(y=j|\vec{x}) &
\end{aligned}
$$

**Cost**

Similar to logistic regression:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& J(\vec{w},b) = \frac{1}{m} \left[loss(a_1,...,a_N,y)\right] \\

& loss(a_1,...,a_N,y) =
\begin{cases}
-\log{a_1} & \text{if } y = 1 \\
-\log{a_2} & \text{if } y = 2 \\
... \\
-\log{a_N} & \text{if } y = N
\end{cases} \\

& (a_1,...,a_N) = g(z_1,...,g_N)

\end{aligned}
$$

## Mutiple Labels Classification

> A classification task associated with each image with multiple lables.  
> Is there a car?  
> Is there a bus?  
> Is there a pedestrian?

![multiple_labels.png](3.1.3%20practical_examples_files/multiple_labels.png)

**Implementation**

```python
model = Sequential([
    Dense(units=25, activation='relu')
    Dense(units=15, activation='relu')
    Dense(units=3, activation='linear') # 3 units representative 3 lables
])
model.compile(loss=BinaryCrossentropy(from_logits=True))
model.fit(X,Y,epochs=100)
logits = model(X)
probabilities  = tf.nn.sigmoid(logits)
predictions = (probabilities > 0.5).numpy().astype(int) # [1,0,1] has_car, not_bus, has_pedestrian
```
